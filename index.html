About FindUganda

Find local. Connect easily. Grow business.

FindUganda is being developed as a Uganda-focused business discovery and promotion platform, beginning in Pader District. Customers will be able to find local businesses and contact them directly. Business owners will be able to manage profiles and branches and promote their services.

Our starting point: Pader District, Uganda.
